# Digital Good Network art commission survey and mark-making

Created by [Stefanie Posavec](http://www.stefanieposavec.com)

Duration: 5 minutes

## About this survey

- **This survey contains:** info about what's in the survey will go here
- The survey should take around 5 minutes to complete.
- **Write as yourself:** respond in your own words, as though you were talking to a friend. No academic language is required!
- **How this data will be used:** info about what I will use this for, how long I will keep it and how it will be credited in the artwork
- **Further questions or concerns?** Please email me at [stef@stefanieposavec.com](mailto:stef@stefanieposavec.com).

## Survey questions

1. Your name — short text, required
2. Email address — email, required
3. Your DGN project — short text
4. Summarise your project findings in language for a lay audience. — long text, max 300 characters
5. In your own words, what does a good digital society look like? — long text, max 300 characters
6. How has your view of the digital good changed since you've done your research? — long text, max 300 characters
7. What advice would you give to future policymakers who want to build the digital good? — long text, max 300 characters

## Mark-making submission

For my artwork, I am collecting small expressive lines and dots, drawn by you directly in the browser. Your contributions will be used to represent human expression and flourishing in my artwork.

No drawing expertise required; this is a way to add your unique contribution to the collective artwork.

**Design guidance:**

- No signatures or any words
- Nothing from real life, please keep your design abstract
- Try to keep your lines from extending off the edge of the artboard

### 1. Long line

Draw a line from the dot on the left to the dot on the right. This line can be as windy or straight, simple or detailed, expressive or minimal as you prefer.
